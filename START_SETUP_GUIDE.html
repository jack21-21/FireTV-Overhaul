 FiveTV Liquid Glass UI Setup

Configured for Jack's JVC 43" (LT-43CF810) • Fire OS 7.7.1 • Govee T3 Sync

Ready for USB Transfer
1

Copy Files to Your USB Flash Drive

Plug your USB stick into your PC and copy the entire contents of this FiveTV Ui folder directly onto the root of the USB drive.

2

Enable "Developer Options" on Your JVC Fire TV

  • On your TV, go to Settings ⚙️ → Device & Software → About.
  • Click your TV Name (Jack's TV) repeatedly 7 times until it says "You are now a developer".
  • Press Back, select Developer Options, and turn Install Unknown Apps to ON.
3

Plug USB into TV & Sideload Apps

Plug the USB drive into the USB port on the back/side of your JVC TV. Open your file manager (or Downloader/X-plore) and install:

Stremio 4K Android TV: Your primary media hub with real-time watch resume.
SmartTube 4K: 100% ad-free YouTube with SponsorBlock built in.

✨ GitHub Over-The-Air (OTA) Live Updates

Once installed, the TV automatically connects to your GitHub repository (jack21-21/FiveTV-Ui) over your home Wi-Fi. Any changes we make on your PC will automatically sync live to your TV—you never need to plug in the USB stick again!
